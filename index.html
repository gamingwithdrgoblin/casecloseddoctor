A medical simulator made by a clinician

Every patient is real medicine.

Take the history, examine in 3D, read the X-ray, and treat while a live physiology model makes your patient better or worse, minute by minute. Then a senior consultant tells you what they would have done.

What you do

From the front door to the debrief

Talk to your patient, examine them, order tests and read the results, make a diagnosis and treat. Get it wrong and they deteriorate, arrest, or die. Every case ends with a graded report and a debrief that cites real clinical guidelines.

For everyone

Civilian difficulty gives you a short list of choices, plain-language feedback and a slower clock. No medical knowledge needed.

For students and clinicians

Doctor and Super-specialist give you the full formulary with search and no hand-holding. Your career runs from FY1 to Consultant.

A story worth finishing

A ten-chapter Story Mode set in Northgate hospital. Chapter 1 is free.

Educational entertainment, not medical advice. Real practice varies and the game simplifies some things to play well. The Kingdom Health Service (KHS) is a fictional parody and is not linked to any real health service.

Why I made this

Hi, I'm Tashif Khan

I'm a doctor, known online as DrGoblin, and I make Case Closed: Doctor myself. I made it because nothing out there was both properly educational and genuinely fun. Medical games were either quizzes or cartoons. I wanted one that felt like a real shift.

I checked every case myself, so the medicine behaves the way it does on a real ward. Patients deteriorate when they would, and drugs work at the doses they really do.

Whether you're a student revising, a clinician keeping sharp, or just curious what happens behind the curtain, I hope it teaches you something and keeps you coming back.
