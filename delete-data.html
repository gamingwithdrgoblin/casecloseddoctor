Data deletion

Privacy · Terms · Delete data · Credits

Last updated 12 November 2026

Case Closed: Doctor has no accounts. Your saves, settings and purchase records are stored only on your device, and we keep no copy.

Delete your game data

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Case Closed: Doctor → Storage.
  2. Tap Clear storage, or uninstall the game.

Advertising data

Ad data is collected by Google AdMob, not by us. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. You can also change your ad consent choice inside the game.

Purchases

Purchase history belongs to your Google account. Manage it at play.google.com.
