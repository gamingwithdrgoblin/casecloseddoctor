This policy covers the Case Closed: Doctor app ("the game") published by DrGoblinDev. Questions: gamingwithmsgoblin@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress, settings and purchase records are saved only on your phone. There are no accounts, and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics code.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and contains medical scenes, including death.

Deleting your data

We hold no personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything, uninstall the game. Full steps: Data deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
